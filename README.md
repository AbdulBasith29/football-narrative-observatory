# football-narrative-observatory
Football Narrative Observatory  A production-grade data engineering and analytics platform for investigating how football narratives evolve across YouTube discourse. 
The platform combines quota-aware ingestion, Snowflake, dbt, NLP, and
interactive analytics to study target-specific opinions, audience dynamics,
channel composition, and narrative evolution around comparable football events.

Current research focus:
Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo.
